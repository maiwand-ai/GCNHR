A SHARED PURPOSE

Where dialogue meets human dignity.

GCNHR is an independent, non-profit association based in Geneva. We create a platform for dialogue, research, documentation and cooperation among Afghan communities, civil society, human rights defenders, legal professionals, researchers and international partners.

WHAT GUIDES OUR WORK

Rights. Dialogue. A fairer future.

Our statutes set out a public-interest mission with particular attention to the situation and voices of the Afghan people.
